Meta (FB & IG) Setup Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to connect your official Globsoft Tech Facebook Page & Instagram Business Account.

1. Connect Instagram to Facebook Page Open your Facebook Page > Settings > Linked Accounts > Connect your Instagram Professional/Business account.
2. Create Meta App Go to developers.facebook.com > My Apps > Create App > Select Business type.
3. Generate Page Access Token Open the Graph API Explorer tool:
  • Select your Meta App and choose Get Page Access Token for Globsoft Tech.
  • Add scopes: pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish.
  • Click Generate Access Token and grant permissions.
4. Paste & Test Paste the Page ID and Access Token into the Meta API Settings modal in this dashboard, click Test Connection, and save!
